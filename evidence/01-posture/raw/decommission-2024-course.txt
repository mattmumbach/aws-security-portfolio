  Sid eb-58950a8c-feb6-11e2-89e0-0800277d041b, a Deny of s3:DeleteBucket to

  DeleteBucket, not DeleteBucketPolicy, so the fix was: delete the bucket

IAM, deliberately retained
  AWSServiceRoleForAutoScaling, AWSServiceRoleForElasticLoadBalancing,
  AWSServiceRoleForRDS   (created Dec 2024, same era, no customer permissions)
  These are service-linked roles. They cannot be removed with delete-role
  (DeleteConflict); they need delete-service-linked-role, and the service
  recreates them the next time it is used. Nothing is gained by removing them.

Lesson worth remembering

  check that actually closed this out was draining every role's CreateDate and
  looking for the year, not searching for names I remembered.
